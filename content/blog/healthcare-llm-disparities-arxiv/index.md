[**Disparities in Multilingual LLM-Based Healthcare Q&A**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17476) is now available on **arXiv**.

## Abstract

This work studies cross-lingual disparities in healthcare question answering by multilingual LLMs. It introduces **MultiWikiHealthCare**, a multilingual dataset built from Wikipedia, and compares both source-level coverage and the factual alignment of model responses across English, German, Turkish, Chinese, and Italian. The analysis shows substantial differences across languages and a systematic tendency for model answers to align more closely with English-language sources. A case study with contextual information and retrieval-augmented generation shows that supplying non-English evidence at inference time can shift responses toward more locally relevant knowledge.
