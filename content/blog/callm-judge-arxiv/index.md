[**A CaLLM Judge is a Better Judge: Mitigating Bias in LLM-as-a-judge**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18108) is now available on **arXiv**.

## Abstract

LLM judges can display systematic preferences unrelated to answer quality, including position, verbosity, and model-family biases. This work reframes such biases as **group-wise miscalibration** and introduces **CaLLM**, a post-hoc multicalibration framework designed to control many bias groups simultaneously, including biases that are not explicitly specified in advance. Theoretical results characterize when representation-based multicalibration bounds these biases and when correcting them can also improve predictive accuracy. Experiments across pairwise preference benchmarks show reductions in multicalibration error for both specified and unspecified bias groups together with improved predictive performance.
