[**Multiclass Local Calibration with the Jensen–Shannon Distance**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26566) is now available on **arXiv**.

## Abstract

Reliable probabilistic classifiers should not only be calibrated globally, but also behave appropriately in local regions of the input space. This work introduces a notion of **multiclass local calibration** and relates it to strong calibration. It analyzes why standard multiclass calibration metrics can fail to detect local miscalibration, especially in sparse regions, and proposes a neural-network regularization approach that aligns predictions with local estimates of class frequencies through the Jensen–Shannon distance. Experiments compare the method with existing multiclass calibration techniques and show that explicitly accounting for locality improves calibration behavior.
