[**Should I Stay or Should I Show? Learning to Selectively Disclose Information**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39818) is now available on **arXiv**.

## Abstract

In many decision-making settings, additional information can help a human, but acquiring or revealing it may be costly and can sometimes even hurt decision quality. This work studies **selective disclosure** under a budget constraint. It characterizes the optimal policy through the conditional **Value of Information**, defined as the reduction in human decision risk caused by disclosure, and treats its estimation as a causal-inference problem. The paper derives guarantees for plug-in policies, proposes practical estimators for classification, and evaluates the framework on benchmark data and in two user studies comparing policy-selected with human-selected disclosure.
