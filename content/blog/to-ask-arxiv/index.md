[**To Ask or Not to Ask: Learning to Require Human Feedback**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08314) is now available on **arXiv**.

## Abstract

Learning to Defer typically treats the human and the model as alternative decision-makers. This work instead studies **Learning to Ask**, where a model can decide both when and how to incorporate additional expert feedback. The framework combines a standard model with an enriched model that has access to expert information, together with a policy for deciding when that extra input is worth acquiring. The paper develops sequential and joint training strategies, establishes consistency guarantees for the joint formulation, and shows on synthetic and real expert data that selectively querying richer human feedback can improve human-AI decision making.
